Delete your data

Yappa has no sign-up, email or password — it uses a random, anonymous player ID — so there's no account to close. Here's what's stored and how it's removed.

Deleted automatically

  • Games (player names, words, guesses, results): deleted when everyone leaves, and always within 24 hours.
  • AI usage counters: deleted after 1 hour.
  • Reports about players in online games: deleted after 90 days.
  • Anonymous player ID: deleted after 30 days without use.

Delete it yourself

Uninstall Yappa, or go to Settings → Apps → Yappa → Storage → Clear data. This removes your name, settings and player ID from your phone.

Ask us to delete it now

Email us with the room code and roughly when you played, and we'll delete that game's data. We'll reply within 30 days.
