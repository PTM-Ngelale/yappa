Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

The short version

  • You don't create an account or give us an email address. Yarnz signs you in with a random, anonymous ID.
  • We store the name you type and what happens in your games — the words, guesses and results — only so the game can be played. Games are deleted within 24 hours.
  • If you choose Play online, you're matched with people you don't know, and they see your name and words.
  • To judge guesses, game words (never your name or ID) are sent to an AI service, Anthropic.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. We only keep daily totals, like how many games were played, with nothing that identifies you. Yarnz doesn't use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Who we are

Yarnz is a word-chain party game for 2 to 5 players, made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy or your data, email preciousngelale@gmail.com.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, anonymous sign-in and server functions. (Supabase privacy policy)
  • Anthropic — its Claude AI judges whether a guess matches the secret word, and whether two chained words make a real phrase. It receives only the words involved (for example the guess, the secret word and its category) — never your name, player ID or room code. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data isn't used to train its models. (Anthropic privacy policy)
  • Expo — delivers app updates. When the app checks for an update, Expo receives technical data such as your device's platform and app version. (Expo privacy policy)
  • Google Play — distributes the app, under Google's own privacy policy.

Other players in your game see your player name, avatar colour and the words and guesses you play. In a game you join through Play online (or a lobby the host opens to online players), those players may be strangers. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Games are deleted when every player has left, and in any case within 24 hours of being created.
  • Usage counters are deleted after one hour.
  • Shared game cards are deleted one year after they're made.
  • Daily game totals are kept, since they contain no personal data.
  • Reports are deleted 90 days after they're made.
  • Anonymous player IDs are deleted after 30 days without use.
  • Data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Server logs are kept by our providers for a limited time under their own policies.

Deleting your data

Because Yarnz has no accounts, there's no sign-up to close. Your data is deleted automatically on the schedule above. You can also:

  • Delete everything on your device by uninstalling Yarnz, or with Settings → Apps → Yarnz → Storage → Clear data.
  • Ask us to delete game data straight away by emailing preciousngelale@gmail.com. Tell us the room code and roughly when you played so we can find it. We'll reply within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how it's used, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us. We rely on providing the game you asked to play (and, for rate limiting and security, our legitimate interest in keeping the service running) as the legal basis for processing.

Playing online

Names and chain words are checked against a list of offensive words, and offensive ones are refused. Any player in an online game can report another from the lobby, the round results or the end of the game. We review reports and may remove offensive content or stop abusive behaviour. Please don't share personal information with players you don't know.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The database only lets players read the games they're in, and the secret word is hidden from the guesser on our server, not just in the app.

Children

Yarnz isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we'll delete it.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. They protect it with safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also say so in the app.
