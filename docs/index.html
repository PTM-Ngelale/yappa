Yappa

Chain words. Guess the secret. Beat the clock.

Yappa is a word-chain party game for three players on their own phones. Two players know a secret word and take turns adding one word to a chain that leads to it — sea → shell → fish. The third player watches the chain grow and tries to guess the secret before time runs out.

Support

How do I start a game?

Everyone taps Local play. One player then taps Create game and shares the 4-letter room code; the other two tap Join with a code. Everyone needs an internet connection — you don't have to be on the same Wi-Fi.

No one to play with?

Tap Play online and we'll match you with two other players. The game starts by itself when the lobby is full. A host can also tap Find players online in the lobby to fill an empty seat.

Someone was offensive

In an online game, tap the flag next to their name in the lobby, or Report a player on the round results or at the end of the game. Reports are reviewed by a person.

A player dropped out

If someone loses their connection, the others can wait for them to come back or end the game.

The AI judged a guess or link wrong

Words are judged by AI and it can occasionally get one wrong. On the round result screen you can tap a link to flag it.
