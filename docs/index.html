Yappa

Chain words. Guess the secret. Beat the clock.

Yappa is a word-chain party game for three players on their own phones. Two players know a secret word and take turns adding one word to a chain that leads to it — sea → shell → fish. The third player watches the chain grow and tries to guess the secret before time runs out.

Support

How do I start a game?

One player taps Create game and shares the 4-letter room code. The other two tap Join with a code. Everyone needs an internet connection — you don't have to be on the same Wi-Fi.

A player dropped out

If someone loses their connection, the others can wait for them to come back or end the game.

The AI judged a guess or link wrong

Words are judged by AI and it can occasionally get one wrong. On the round result screen you can tap a link to flag it.
